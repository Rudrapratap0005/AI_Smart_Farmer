About Us

AI Smart Farmer is a modern digital platform designed to support farmers using advanced technologies like Artificial Intelligence and real-time data analysis.

Our mission is to empower farmers with smart tools that help improve crop yield, reduce risks, and make better agricultural decisions.

🌱 Our platform provides:
• AI-based crop recommendations
• Real-time weather updates
• Soil health insights
• Crop disease detection
• Farm analytics dashboard

We believe technology can transform agriculture and create a smarter, more sustainable future for farmers.
